About the Founder

Inspired by the compassion of his late father, Mathew Olaniyi Amudipe, Dr. Adebayo Amudipe established MOAF to continue a legacy of generosity, faith, and service.

Growing up in Ondo State, Dr. Amudipe witnessed the devastating impact of poverty on neighbors who simply needed an advocate. Today, he leads a team of volunteers, medical professionals, and community leaders that bring practical aid to people who would otherwise be overlooked.

  • Founded in 2012 after years of informal outreach by the Amudipe family.
  • Registered non-profit operating across Ondo, Lagos, Ekiti, and Oyo States.
  • Governed by a board of faith-driven professionals committed to transparency.

Programs & Impact

Our multi-pronged initiatives target the root causes of poverty while responding to urgent needs.

Community Health Clinics

Mobile clinics offer prenatal care, vaccinations, cancer screenings, and essential medicines to remote villages.

  • 12,000+ patients treated since 2018
  • Telehealth follow-ups for chronic illnesses

Education & Scholarships

Tuition aid, digital labs, and mentoring for high-potential students who would otherwise drop out.

  • STEM bootcamps for girls
  • School feeding partnerships

Women's Economic Empowerment

Microgrants, cooperative savings, and business coaching help widows and single mothers launch sustainable ventures.

  • 420 businesses launched
  • 80% repayment rate reinvested into grants

Food & Relief Network

Rapid-response teams deliver meals, hygiene kits, and psychosocial support during disasters and seasonal hunger.

  • Warehouse partners in 3 states
  • Community kitchens run by volunteers
